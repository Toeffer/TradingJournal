## Finviz free-tier workflow

Suggested Finviz screen to replicate manually:
- Price above $2
- Average volume above 500k
- Relative volume above 2
- Current change between roughly +3% and +15%
- New high / above 20-day moving average / strong technical setup
- Exclude names that are already +40% to +100% unless you explicitly want ultra-high risk
