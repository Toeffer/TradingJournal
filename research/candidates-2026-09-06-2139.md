## Run-blocking finding: the brief's evidence standard is not executable in this environment

This is the most important output of this run, and it is a process finding rather than a market one.

`RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` imposes two hard gates. Both are currently unsatisfiable for any name outside the
single-row snapshot, which means **no candidate can mechanically reach `trigger_ready` regardless of
how good it is**:

1. **Primary-source date verification is impossible.** The brief requires that "the synthesizing pass
   must open the primary source itself" for every non-rejected catalyst date, and `AGENTS.md` requires
   classifying `secondary_only` dates as Reject. Every issuer domain attempted in this run was refused:
   - `www.aehr.com` → `EGRESS_BLOCKED` by the network egress proxy
   - `www.flatexdegiro.com` → `EGRESS_BLOCKED` by the network egress proxy
   - `investors.aehr.com`, `investors.aerovironment.com` → DNS `ENOTFOUND`
   - Quartr MCP (the repo's primary-source filings/events route) → `subscription_required`; the signed-in
     account is on plan `none`
   Only `WebSearch` returns results, and search snippets are explicitly tier-3/tier-5 sources that
   "cannot verify a final date."
2. **Evidence-based price levels are impossible.** `AGENTS.md` requires "evidence-based levels rather
   than broker-page day-low proxies," and `SETUPS.md` requires a structural entry, invalidation and
   first target. No multi-session OHLC source is reachable:
   - FMP `chart` (historical EOD) → `ACCESS DENIED`, requires a higher plan
   - FMP `quote` / `batch-quote` → `ACCESS DENIED`, requires Premium or above
   - `stooq.com` (the source the repo's own EU scanner uses) → proxy `403` on CONNECT
   - `query1.finance.yahoo.com` → proxy `403` on CONNECT
   What still works is FMP `company/profile-symbol` (last close, market cap, average share volume) and
   FMP `marketPerformance`. Those are enough to run the **universe and liquidity gates** — which is how
   this run rejected six of eight leads on hard numbers — but a single last-close price cannot produce a
   breakout level, a pullback zone, a structural stop, or a defensible planned R.

**Consequence.** The routine has now printed **NO NEW TRADE for eight consecutive weeks**
(2026-07-19 through 2026-09-06). That streak should not be read as eight weeks of honest market
discipline. It is substantially a data-access artifact: since 2026-08-23 the canonical snapshot has
carried exactly **1 row** for three straight weeks (it held 13 rows on 2026-07-12), and the two gates
above close off every route to a setup for names not in that row.

The scanner itself is **not** broken — `.github/workflows/scanner.yml` and `scanner-eu.yml` are firing
on schedule and committing daily through 2026-09-04. It is simply recording almost nothing: 25 signals
in July, 15 in August, 1 in September. Whether that reflects a genuinely quiet tape or thresholds that
have drifted too tight is a question for the human, not for this run.

Recommended human action, in priority order:

1. Decide whether the FMP plan should cover `chart`/`quote`, or whether the egress policy should allow
   `stooq.com` — either one restores structural levels.
2. Decide whether issuer IR domains should be allowed through the egress proxy — without that, the
   primary-source gate can never pass and every catalyst is a mandatory Reject.
3. Review `scanner/config.toml` thresholds against the 1-row snapshot. Note separately that
   `research/scanner-summary.md` shows the scanner's own realized edge is negative across the 60-69,
   70-79 and 80+ buckets at every horizon, so loosening thresholds is not obviously the right fix.

Until at least (1) and (2) are resolved, this routine can produce Reject decisions and universe
screening honestly, but it cannot produce a qualified entry.
