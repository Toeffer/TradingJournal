## Red-team conclusion

vendor, a news snippet, or a prior-year analog is `secondary_only`, and promoting on it is precisely the failure mode
the primary-source gate exists to prevent. With no channel to open a primary source this run, no bear case had to be
weakened to reach REJECT on every name; the honest verdict is a documented no-candidate week plus a clean, real-data
lead list for human follow-up.
