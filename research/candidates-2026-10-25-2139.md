## Blocker — the routine could not verify anything this week

This is a tooling failure, not a market finding, and it is the single reason every lead was
rejected. Three required capabilities were unavailable:

1. **No primary source could be opened.** The session's network policy denied `CONNECT` to
   every issuer-IR, exchange and market-data host attempted — stooq.com, finance.yahoo.com,
   query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com, www.boerse-frankfurt.de, api.nasdaq.com,
   www.alphavantage.co, api.twelvedata.com, financialmodelingprep.com, eodhd.com,
   api.marketdata.app, www.investing.com. The page-fetch tool additionally had **no DNS
   resolution at all** and returned `ENOTFOUND` for every host, including sec.gov. Only the
   web-search tool worked, and it returns summaries, which `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` classifies as
   leads that "cannot verify a final date". Every date below is therefore `secondary_only` at
   best, and `verification.md` admits only `verified` dates to Actionable or Early Watch.
2. **eToro tradeability could not be verified.** `ETORO_TRADEABILITY.md` makes unverifiable
   broker availability an outright rejection, independent of everything else.
3. **No structured market data.** The canonical snapshot is empty and no dated provider was
   reachable, so market cap, liquidity tier and multi-session structure were all unavailable.
   With no 20-day high, swing low or ATR, any entry, stop or target would have been invented
   rather than derived — so no `enter_if_triggered` row was possible for any name on any
   evidence.

The FMP and Quartr data servers were advertised at session start but exposed no tools, so
neither could substitute.

**What would fix it:** allow the issuer-IR and market-data hosts above in the environment's
network policy, restore DNS for the page-fetch tool, or re-point the scanner so
`scanner_signals.csv` is fresher than the 7-day lookback. Until then this routine can produce
a documented lead pool but cannot clear its own evidence gate, and will return NO NEW TRADE
every week regardless of what the market offers.
