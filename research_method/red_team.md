# Stage 4 — Red team

Assume each surviving idea is wrong. The red-team pass is separate from the analyst who
first liked the candidate whenever the tooling permits.

## Required attacks

For every candidate ask:

- Is the catalyst date, mechanism, or source wrong?
- Did the event already occur or move?
- Is the event incremental rather than material?
- Is the expected good news already reflected in price and estimates?
- Is the setup merely a high-beta or sector-sympathy move?
- Can financing, dilution, insider selling, a lockup release, or an offering overwhelm it?
- Does the stop represent real invalidation, or just an arbitrary nearby number?
- Can an overnight gap make the proposed risk arithmetic meaningless?
- Is liquidity adequate at the intended entry and exit times, not just on average?
- Is there an intervening earnings report or macro event before the stated catalyst?
- Would this still qualify without the scanner score or thematic story?

## Verdicts

Use one:

- `SURVIVE`: evidence and setup remain intact.
- `DOWNGRADE_EARLY_WATCH`: event is real but the current setup is premature.
- `REJECT`: evidence, tradeability, expectations, or risk invalidates the idea.

A candidate cannot remain Actionable when the red-team verdict is not `SURVIVE`.
Record the strongest rejection argument and why the candidate survived it. Do not weaken
bear cases to preserve a desired shortlist size.
